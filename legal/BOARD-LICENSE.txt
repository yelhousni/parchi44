src/components/board-art.ts contains the visible artwork with the Adobe metadata/wrapper removed and Arial Bold selected for cross-platform rendering, and the eight neutral safe squares shaded gray. The four colored starting squares retain their original colors. The board is rotated at runtime; interactive game pieces and name labels are overlaid.
